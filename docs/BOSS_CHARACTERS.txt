DRAGON BALL Z: SUPERSONIC WARRIORS 2 - Godot port
BOSS CHARACTERS (cheat on settings page 3)
==========================================

How to use
  1. Turn on BOSS CHARACTERS (settings menu, page 3: CHEATS 2).
  2. In a character select screen (Free Battle - both teams -, Z-Battle, Maximum), hold L (A key on the
     keyboard) while you pick a character from the list below (X key = A button, or tap the portrait).
  3. A small skull appears on the portrait. In battle the skull is on the fighter's panel (lower screen).
  Characters that are not in the list are picked normally.

What a boss is
  The game has no separate boss characters: a boss is the normal fighter with a "boss level" (1-3) that the
  game gives to computer opponents in Z-Battle and Maximum mode. The level selects a stronger parameter
  block from the character's own data:
    Level 1 ... 21000 HP (normal fighters have about 13000-18500), other stats unchanged
    Level 2 ... 21000 HP, stronger stats, super attacks cost 25% ki instead of 50%
    Level 3 ... 26000 HP (SSJ Goku and Cell: 32000), stronger stats, ki costs halved, super attacks free
  Three level-3 bosses also have their own boss-only moves (special ki blasts, e.g. "15 RUSH", and boss
  versions of their super attacks): Final Boss SSJ Goku, Boss Cell and Boss Broly.
  The cheat gives each character the highest boss level the original game uses for it.
